Executive Summary

The Core Insight

Net worth ignores what makes people truly wealthy—their experiences.

A person who has traveled the world, attended historic events, and built meaningful memories is told by every financial app that they're "worth" the same as someone who simply hoarded cash.

Our Solution

An app that quantifies the value of life experiences using a proprietary AI valuation algorithm, integrates with financial aggregators (Plaid), and combines both to display a user's "Total Life Worth."

Section 2

The Problem

Every financial app tells the same incomplete story: your worth = assets minus liabilities.

What Net Worth Misses

Consider two people with identical $500,000 net worth:

Person A

  • Saved aggressively, never traveled
  • Never attended a concert
  • No meaningful experiences logged
  • High savings, low living

Person B

  • Visited 47 countries
  • Saw historic events live
  • Met their spouse backpacking
  • Rich life, moderate savings

Traditional metrics say they're equal. They're not even close.

The Real Costs

👤
For Consumers
78% of Millennials prefer experiences over things
  • No tool helps track, value, or optimize experiential investments
  • FOMO based purely on visible consumption
  • No framework for understanding if experiences are "worth it"
💼
For Financial Advisors
Losing younger clients to robo-advisors
  • Clients ask "Can I afford this trip?" but advisors only see balance sheets
  • No tools to incorporate experiential goals into planning
  • Wealth management differentiation is nearly impossible

For Experience Providers
Competing purely on price
  • Can't demonstrate lasting value of experiences
  • Customer loyalty programs feel transactional, not meaningful
  • No way to prove ROI on "experience" marketing claims
Section 3

The Solution

Introducing Life Worth™ — a comprehensive view of personal wealth.

The Formula

Life Worth = Financial Net Worth + Experience Value

Core Product Features

1. AI Experience Valuation Engine

Users input experiences (concert, trip, milestone). AI considers rarity, personal significance, social proof, and market comparables to assign monetary value.

Example: "Attending the 2024 Super Bowl" = $4,200 base + $800 personal factors

2. Financial Aggregation (Plaid Integration)

Connect bank accounts, investments, crypto wallets. Real-time net worth tracking with a unified dashboard showing the complete picture.

3. Partner Experience NFTs

Travel companies, airlines, and venues issue verified experience NFTs — proof of attendance with automatic value attribution.

4. $LIFE Token Economy

Earn tokens by adding experiences, referring friends, booking through partners. Spend tokens to boost valuations, unlock premium features, redeem with partners.

5. Social Layer

Share Life Worth (or keep private). Opt-in leaderboards by category. "Experience portfolios" to inspire others.

The "Aha" Moment

"Your experiences are worth more than your savings account"

That single insight reframes their entire relationship with money and living.

Section 11

Risk Factors & Mitigation

AI valuation seen as arbitrary

Mitigation: Transparency in methodology, user feedback loops, third-party validation studies.

Competitor copies concept

Mitigation: First-mover advantage, network effects compound with data, B2B lock-in creates switching costs.

Token classified as security

Mitigation: Securities counsel from day 1, utility-only design, no trading at launch.

Unable to hire CTO

Mitigation: Aggressive comp package, meaningful equity (8-12%), leverage founder network.

Economic downturn reduces experience spending

Mitigation: Pivot messaging to "past experiences have lasting value" — nostalgia plays well in downturns.

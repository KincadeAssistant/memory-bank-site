Recommendation

For Instagram: Gold Gradient + Dark Stacked Layers

The stacked layers icon on gold gradient background is the strongest choice:

  • Reads well at small sizes (IG profile is tiny)
  • Gold stands out in a sea of blue/white app icons
  • The layers concept is unique and memorable
  • Works as app icon too for consistency
